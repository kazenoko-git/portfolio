## The question

What does it take to move an idea about computation into silicon?

This project follows a first neuromorphic NPU design for Hack Club ASIC. This page will collect the motivation, architecture, implementation decisions, and verification evidence as they become ready to share.

## Current work

The design report is being prepared. The repository documentation below tracks the Talos 2 branch; the longer writeup will connect the design decisions to verification evidence.

[Read the report outline →](/writing/neuromorphic-npu)

## What will be documented

- The target workload and why it calls for dedicated hardware.
- The numerical representation and the contract between the reference model and RTL.
- The datapath, memory, control, and communication interfaces.
- The verification approach, measured results, and remaining limitations.
