## The question

What does it take to move an idea about computation into silicon?

This project began with Talos 1: a sixteen-neuron spiking reservoir built to learn how a temporal computing idea becomes real RTL. Its completed design report explains the circuit, the simulation evidence, and the limits of the toy benchmarks.

## Current work

The report is specifically about **Talos 1**, the small time-multiplexed design and FPGA-board learning stage. Talos 2 is a separate follow-on design, not the chip described in the report.

[Read the Talos 1 report →](/writing/neuromorphic-npu)

## What will be documented

- Talos 1's neuron state, fixed recurrent connections, and shared arithmetic unit.
- Its SPI controls and what a host can inspect.
- What the simulation benchmarks establish, where they take shortcuts, and what should be tested next.
